Technical Module

Get Ahead in Your Career with SAP Technical Skills That Are in Demand

Our SAP Technical Module training is designed to give you a strong understanding of how to build, customize, and configure SAP systems through hands-on, real-world examples.

This program is ideal for both beginner programmers and IT professionals who want to enhance their technical expertise and stay competitive in the SAP ecosystem.

💡 What Are SAP Technical Modules?

SAP Technical Modules focus on the technical aspects of SAP systems, enabling you to develop, customize, and integrate SAP solutions based on business requirements.

These modules help businesses to:

  • Develop custom reports and applications
  • Enhance standard SAP functionalities
  • Integrate SAP with external systems
  • Manage data and system performance efficiently

👉 In short, SAP Technical Modules are the backbone that enable SAP systems to function, adapt, and scale with business needs.

📘 What You Will Learn

  • SAP ABAP programming (reports, forms, enhancements)
  • Data Dictionary (tables, structures, views)
  • Debugging and performance optimization
  • Smart Forms and Adobe Forms
  • User exits and enhancements
  • Basics of SAP integration and interfaces

You will also gain hands-on experience by working on real-time SAP development projects.

🔗 How Technical Modules Support Business Processes

SAP Technical Modules work closely with functional modules to deliver complete business solutions:

  • Support modules like SAP SD, MM, FI with custom developments
  • Enable automation and system enhancements
  • Integrate SAP with third-party applications using interfaces
  • Improve system performance and usability
